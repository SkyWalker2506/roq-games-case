## Reported, not fixed

| What | Measurement | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| Counter can read 6/5 | `Case3Director.PushStack` increments `StackCard.Collected` with no ceiling, and `Case3PageEntries` hands the Noodle card 6 collectibles and the Sweets card 6 against `stackRequirement = 5`. Collect them all and the card prints 6/5. Cat has 2 and cannot. Reproduced mechanically: `Case3PageEntries.Build` now logs `POPULATION Noodle: 6 collectible(s) against a requirement of 5` and a `STACK_OVERFLOW` error for each offending card | The population is authored in `Case3PageEntries` (this pass's file) but the requirement is a single field on `Case3Director` (another pass's file). The honest fix is a per-card requirement on `Case3Director.StackCard`, so the violation is reported with its exact numbers instead of being clamped - clamping would print 5/5 with six items on the card, a wrong number in place of an impossible one |
| Dim tone reads darker than the reference's | `Case3_PageObjectDim` is a flat linear value multiply at `_Value` 0.238, derived in an earlier round from the reference at linear ratios 0.227 and 0.234 | The number is measured and the owner has not raised it. Changing `_Value` would break a measured value to chase an unmeasured impression; it needs its own measurement pass against a matched region. |
